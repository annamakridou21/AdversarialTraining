# CV project entry

**Adversarial Training via Min-Max Optimization**  
Python, PyTorch | Team course project, University of Crete  
[Project repository](https://github.com/annamakridou21/AdversarialTraining)

- Co-developed a PyTorch framework for CIFAR-10 classification with PGD attacks and mixed adversarial training.
- Evaluated a matched clean control and adversarial fine-tuning on 10,000 test images; at epsilon 4/255, PGD-20 robust accuracy was 35.82% versus 1.14%, with clean accuracy of 70.59% versus 78.70% (five restarts, seed 42).

## Shorter alternative

- Co-developed and evaluated a CIFAR-10 adversarial-training framework in PyTorch, comparing clean and robust accuracy under PGD attacks with matched training budgets and validation-based checkpoint selection.
